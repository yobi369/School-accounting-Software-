Before real personal data is processed, the parties must execute appropriate privacy/data-processing terms covering at least: documented processing instructions, purpose and lawful basis, data categories and data subjects, confidentiality, access control, safeguards, subprocessors, security incidents and notifications, assistance with data-subject requests, retention and deletion, audits where appropriate, cross-border transfers where applicable, and return/export of data at termination.
